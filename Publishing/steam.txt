[h1]Pyrinth Bionics[/h1]

Add-on for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3336544632]Epochs - Pyrinth[/url], adding bionic implants powered by Pyrinth.

[h2]Features[/h2]

Pyrinth-based, fire-themed bionic implants with abilities and a new resource to power them.

Beware: you're playing with fire! Pawns can easily get heatstroke, and stacking all implants may overheat them even below freezing.

[h3]Implants[/h3]

[list]
    [*]Pyrinth Eye - A beautiful eye that gives a pawn an edge in social situations, granting the ability to seduce pawns and influence prisoners and slaves.
    [*]Pyrinth Arm - An arm that gives the pawn the ability to cauterize wounds, turning bleeding into burns and burning off bleeding stumps. It also warms the target up, either reducing the severity of hypothermia or giving the pawn heatstroke.
    [*]Pyrinth Leg - A leg that gives the pawn the ability to pyrinth jump.
    [*]Pyrinth Liver - A bionic liver that gives the pawn the ability to purge contaminants, diseases, and parasites.
    [*]Pyrinth Stomach - (With Royalty) A bionic stomach lined with Pyrinth that sterilizes food, making digestion a bit more efficient by cooking ingested matter. It can prevent gastrointestinal diseases.
[/list]

[h3]Extras[/h3]

[list]
    [*]Pyrinth potion - refills Pyrinth Heat and staves off cold.
[/list]

[list]
    [*]Pyrinth implants give Pyromaniac pawns a mood bonus.
    [*]Pyrinth implants also heat up pawns, making them vulnerable to hot climates but more resilient to cold.
    [*]Pyrinth Heat is the resource for Pyrinth implant abilities.
[/list]
